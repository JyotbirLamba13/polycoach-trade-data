WHAT POLYALPHA DOES

Not predictions. Post-mortems.

Everyone guesses what happens next. We show you what already happened: who won, who lost, and exactly how, on every resolved Polymarket market, traced to real on-chain money.

Real outcomes, not vibes

Every market's true resolution, open and close dates, volume and live odds, pulled straight from Polymarket. No estimates, no guesses.

Real wallets, real P&L

We compute actual profit and loss per wallet from 293M on-chain trades. See exactly who made the money, who lost it, and link to their profile.

Searchable and indexed

13,500 markets, each with its own deep-dive page. Find any event, study the smart money, and cite a real, linkable source.

OUR THESIS

Prediction markets are the most honest dataset in finance: real people, real money, public outcomes. The edge isn't guessing the future. It's studying who reads it right, how early they enter, and how much they risk. We turn millions of on-chain trades into that lesson.
